Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026

This policy explains what the Glitch Sports iPhone game ("the game", "we", "us") collects, why, who else receives it, and how you can delete it. The game is made by Glitch Sports, a sole proprietorship registered in British Columbia, Canada.

The short version

  • You never create an account and never give us your name or email address. The game gives you a random, anonymous player id.
  • We store your game progress, gameplay events, crash/diagnostic data and any problem reports you send, linked to that anonymous id.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when iOS asks.
  • Apple handles all payments. We never see your card or Apple ID details.
  • Settings → Cloud save → Delete my data deletes what our server keeps about you.

1. Your anonymous player id

The first time the game goes online it signs you in anonymously and gets a random player id (a UUID). It isn't based on your name, email, phone number or device identifiers. The sign-in session is stored in your iPhone's Keychain, so it survives deleting and reinstalling the game on the same phone (and can come with you if you restore a new phone from an encrypted backup). Everything below that we store on our server is linked to this id.

2. What we collect and why

Game progress (cloud save)

A copy of your saved game: trophies, unlocked and equipped items, best scores and stats per mode, Glitch Run progress, progress in hidden modes, login days, daily-challenge streak and coupons. It's uploaded a few seconds after you save (never during a game) so it can be restored and kept in sync. Purpose: running the game (backup and restore).

Device and app facts

Each time the game connects: your device's hardware model (for example "iPhone17,1", never its name), iOS version (in diagnostics), the app version, your App Store country (from Apple's storefront, not from your location) and your device language, plus when your player id was created and last seen. Purpose: running the game, compatibility and fixing problems.

Gameplay events (analytics)

A compact log of what happens in the game, for example: a session started or ended (and how long it lasted), a game or run finished (mode, score, makes, perfects, distance, zone reached), an item unlocked and how, a secret found, a level or boss fight in a hidden mode finished, a daily reward or challenge claimed, an ad watched (type and where in the game), a gift claimed, a purchase made (which product). Events are queued on your phone and sent in batches, and wait while Low Data Mode is on. Purpose: understanding how the game is played so we can balance and improve it, and to send in-game gifts to groups of players (for example, "players stuck on a level").

Crash and diagnostic data

When the game crashes, hangs or runs into an error, it records:

  • Crash and hang reports from Apple's MetricKit: the type of problem, a short technical description, the top lines of the crash trace (memory offsets inside the app), the app version, iOS version and device model.
  • Daily performance metrics from MetricKit: launch and resume time, hang time, time in the foreground, peak memory and the number of unexpected exits.
  • Error logs for things that went wrong without a crash (for example a failed download), with a short fixed message and a few technical details.
  • Breadcrumbs: the last ~20 steps before the problem, as short labels only, such as which screen was open, which mode was being played, a score or zone number, or an item id. Breadcrumbs never contain text you typed.

Any email address or long number (7 or more digits) that ends up in an error description is replaced on your phone before it is stored. Diagnostics are queued on your phone and sent when you're online and not in a game. Purpose: finding and fixing bugs and crashes.

"Report a problem" messages

If you use Settings → Help → Report a problem, we receive:

  • Your message (up to 500 characters). Before it leaves your phone, email addresses are replaced with "[email]" and runs of 7 or more digits (like phone or card numbers) with "[number]". Please don't include your name or other personal details anyway.
  • A small summary of your progress (trophies, number of items, games played per mode, best run distance, levels and boss fights completed, login days).
  • The app version, iOS version and device model, and a reference code (like GS-7K2QXM) shown to you.
  • If "Include recent activity" is on (the default): your recent breadcrumbs and the last few errors the game noticed. Turn it off to send only the items above.

Purpose: answering and fixing the problem you reported. We may also send you an in-game gift through the game in response; we can't reply to you directly unless you email us.

Purchase records

Purchases are made through Apple. After a purchase, the game sends Apple's signed receipt for it to our server, which checks it with Apple and records the product, transaction ids, purchase date, store environment and whether it was refunded. We never receive your name, Apple ID, payment card or billing address. Purpose: preventing fraud and restoring purchases correctly when accounts are linked. What you own in the game is always read from Apple's own records on your phone.

Game Center (optional)

If you're signed in to Game Center, your achievements, high scores and best streaks are sent to Apple's Game Center, where leaderboards may show them to other players under your Game Center nickname, according to your Game Center settings. Apple handles this data under Apple's privacy policy. We also store your Game Center team player id (a game-specific id from Apple, not your nickname) next to your anonymous player id, so we can offer to restore your saved game on a new phone. A restore is only done after the game checks Apple's signature proving you are that Game Center player.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

If you choose Settings → Cloud save → Save my progress, you sign in with Apple. The game asks Apple for no name and no email; we only receive Apple's stable, app-specific user id, which we link to your anonymous player id so you can get your progress back on another phone.

Gifts and remote settings

The game checks our server for gifts (free items, trophies or coupons we send to players) and for game settings we can tune without an app update. We record which gifts you've claimed.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or your device's advertising identifier. We don't sell personal data.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos you choose to watch, and full-screen ads between games (never during a game). Google's software in the game collects information to show, measure and protect ads, which can include your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), your IP address and the coarse location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses it as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and some other regions): the game shows Google's consent message before ads load. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices (shown where it applies).
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency): iOS asks whether Glitch Sports may track you. If you tap "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier isn't shared and you'll still see ads, just less personalised ones. You can change this any time in the iOS Settings app (also reachable from Settings → Privacy → Tracking & app settings in the game).
  • Family-safe ads: the game asks Google for ads suitable for general audiences (rating G), and sensitive ad categories are blocked.
  • Remove Ads: buying Remove Ads (or the Everything Bundle) stops full-screen ads. Rewarded ads stay optional.

The ad events we log ourselves (section 2) record only that an ad was watched and where, never the advertising identifier.

4. Who else receives data

  • Supabase hosts our server and database (anonymous player id, cloud save, events, diagnostics, problem reports, purchase records). Supabase processes this data on our behalf. As part of handling network requests its infrastructure may log your IP address; we don't store IP addresses in our database.
  • Google (AdMob) receives the ad data described in section 3.
  • Apple processes payments, Game Center and Sign in with Apple under its own privacy policy, and provides the crash reports and metrics (MetricKit) the game forwards to us.
  • Resend delivers a daily alert email to us containing only totals (for example the number of crashes and players affected and error types), never player ids or messages.

We may also disclose data if required by law, or to protect the game and its players from fraud or abuse.

5. How long we keep it

Gameplay events, crash and error reports, performance metrics and problem reports are deleted automatically 13 months after they were recorded. Your player id, cloud save, purchase records and gift claims are kept for as long as your player id exists, so your progress can always be restored, until you delete them with "Delete my data". Data kept on your phone stays there until you delete the game (the anonymous sign-in stays in your Keychain even then).

6. Delete my data

In the game: Settings → Cloud save → Delete my data. It deletes your anonymous player id, any older ids of yours merged into it when you restored a save, and everything our server has linked to them: your cloud save, gameplay events, purchase records, Game Center and Sign in with Apple links, gift claims, crash and error reports, performance metrics and problem reports. It can't be undone. The game also forgets the sign-in on your phone and clears its queued events and diagnostics. Optionally, "Also reset this device" starts your game over on that phone too.

  • Your purchases stay with your Apple ID: use Restore purchases to get them back.
  • Game Center scores and achievements are Apple's; manage them in the iOS Settings app (Game Center).
  • If you can't use the button (for example you deleted the game), email us at glitch.sports.info@gmail.com. Because we don't know who you are, please include a "Report a problem" reference code or your Game Center nickname so we can find the right player.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict some uses, and to withdraw consent. Use "Delete my data" or email glitch.sports.info@gmail.com (see section 6 on identifying your player). You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on: performance of our agreement with you to run the game and cloud save; our legitimate interests in improving the game, fixing bugs and preventing fraud (analytics, diagnostics, purchase checks); and your consent for personalised ads (collected through Google's consent message).

We are based in British Columbia, Canada. Canadian players are covered by Canada's privacy laws (PIPEDA, and British Columbia's PIPA); you can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia.

8. Children

Glitch Sports is rated 4+ on the App Store and is meant for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. We don't ask for names, emails or ages, ads are limited to general-audience (G-rated) content, and sensitive ad categories are blocked. If you are a parent and think your child has sent us information, use "Delete my data" on their device or email us and we'll delete it.

9. Where data is processed

Our server is hosted by Supabase in the United States (AWS us-east-1, North Virginia), so data from players in Canada and elsewhere is stored there and may be accessible to U.S. authorities under U.S. law. Google and Apple process data in many countries. When data moves outside your country, we rely on our providers' safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). On our server each player can only write their own data and can't read anyone else's; only we can view it, through a private admin page. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we collect small for that reason.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If a change is significant (for example collecting a new kind of data), we'll also tell you in the game or in the App Store release notes.
