Support

If you already sent a report from the game, include its reference code (like GS-7K2QXM).

Purchases

How do I restore my purchases?

Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID you bought with, then tap Restore purchases in either place:

  • Settings → Purchases → Restore purchases
  • the bottom of the Shop (the bag icon on Home)

The game lists what it restored, or says "No purchases to restore" if that Apple ID has none.

I was charged but didn't get my item

Try Restore purchases first (above). Purchases waiting for approval (for example Ask to Buy) unlock as soon as they're approved. For refunds, use Apple's reportaproblem.apple.com; we can't issue refunds ourselves.

Ads

How do I remove ads?

Buy Remove Ads in Settings → Purchases or in the Shop (the Everything Bundle includes it too). It stops all full-screen ads for good. Rewarded ads (the ones you choose to watch for an extra) stay available, and you keep all your trophies.

When do ads show?

Never during a game. Full-screen ads only appear when you tap Play again or go back to the menu after a game or run, and new players don't see any for their first couple of sessions. Ads are limited to family-friendly content.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where your region requires it, Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices reopens Google's consent form. To allow or stop tracking, use Settings → Privacy → Tracking & app settings, which opens the game's page in the iOS Settings app.

Your progress

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

Your progress backs up to the cloud automatically after each game (check Settings → Cloud save; it should say "Saved to the cloud"). To get it on a new phone, link it one of two ways on your old phone first:

  1. Game Center: be signed in to Game Center on the old phone and open the game while online, so your save is linked. On the new phone, sign in to the same Game Center account and open the game: Home shows "Saved game found". Tap Restore. (You can also find Restore saved game in Settings → Cloud save.)
  2. Sign in with Apple: on the old phone, tap Settings → Cloud save → Save my progress and sign in. On the new phone, tap Save my progress again with the same Apple ID: your saved game is merged in. No name or email is shared with us.

When two saves meet, nothing is lost: for every stat and item, the higher progress wins. Purchases come back separately with Restore purchases.

I reinstalled the game. Is my progress gone?

Usually not. The game remembers your anonymous player on this iPhone even after you delete it, so after reinstalling, open the game while online and your cloud save comes back. If it doesn't, use the Game Center or Sign in with Apple steps above.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Cloud save → Delete my data. This deletes everything our server keeps about you (anonymous player id, cloud save, gameplay history, gifts and purchase records). It can't be undone. Turn on Also reset this device if you want to start the game over on this phone too. Your purchases stay with your Apple ID. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Can't open the game any more? Email glitch.sports.info@gmail.com with a "Report a problem" reference code or your Game Center nickname so we can find your data.

Problems

How do I report a bug?

In the game: Settings → Help → Report a problem. Tell us what happened in a sentence or two. Leave Include recent activity on to send the last screens you visited and any errors the game noticed (no names or emails), which helps us a lot. You'll get a reference code like GS-7K2QXM. It works offline too: the report sends itself next time you're online.

Please don't put your name, email or other personal details in the message.

The game froze or crashed

Crash reports reach us automatically, so we usually know. If it keeps happening, restart your iPhone, update to the latest version of the game, and send a Report a problem describing what you were doing.

Achievements or leaderboards aren't updating

Check you're signed in to Game Center (iOS Settings app → Game Center). The game sends achievements and scores when you're back on the menus, not mid-game. Settings → Game Center in the game shows your awards and ranks.
